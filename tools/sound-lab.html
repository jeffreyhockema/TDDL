TDDL Sound Lab

Every sound the game can make, on demand. Serve the project (npm start) and open /tools/sound-lab.html — opening the file directly will not work, because ES modules need HTTP.

Click anything once to start audio. The samples are the Doom Sound Bulb pack (see sounds/README.md). The listener slider moves you left or right of a sound placed at the centre, to hear the panning and falloff.
